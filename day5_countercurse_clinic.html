Chunk 1 · The protocolE5-PRO-TR-?? ⚠

Five steps, every time

The Counter-Curse Protocol: Symptom (what actually happened) → Predict the cause → Trace to the exact line → One change → Re-run. The protocol exists so you never change three things at once. Try it on a symptom you already know:

SYMPTOM: an apprentice's spell prints  121212
         when the answer should be     36
Predict You met this exact curse on Day 1. Predict the cause, and name the one change that lifts it, before revealing.
The text trap: a number arrived through input() as text, and text times 3 repeats the letters. The one change: wrap the input in int() and convert at the gate. If you diagnosed that from the symptom alone, you already think like clinic staff.
Chunk 2 · The shared cursed spellE5-PRO-TR-?? ⚠

Two loud curses

The whole room breaks this together. Copy it into clinic.py exactly as written, curses and all. It's the prize-draw spell for tonight's Demonstration, and it carries four curses: two loud, two silent. Read it, trace it, circle your suspects. Then run, and lift the loud ones first, one change per run.

guests = ["Mira", "Sam", "Ravi", "June"]

print("Guests arriving for the Demonstration:")
for i in range(1, 4):
    print(i, guests[i])

tickets = input("How many tickets sold? ")
total = tickets * 5
print(f"Gold earned: {total}")

winner = random.choice(guests)
print(f"The door prize goes to {winer}!")
Predict Type 4 when it asks. Which line misfires first, and what will the traceback's last line say? (Careful: the first crash may be further down than you think.)
The spell runs surprisingly far (greeting, three guests, the question, even a gold line) and then crashes at the prize draw: NameError: name 'random' is not defined, because the chance-magic was never imported. One change, import random at the top, and re-run. It crashes again one line later: NameError: name 'winer' is not defined. The vessel was filled as winner but poured as winer. Fix the spelling. Two loud curses lifted, two runs, two log rows. A loud curse at least tells you where it lives.

The gold that lies

No traceback this time, but look at the gold line. Tickets cost 5 gold each and 4 were sold. The spell says 44444.

SYMPTOM: Gold earned: 44444
EXPECTED: Gold earned: 20
Predict Run the protocol on paper: predict the cause, name the line, name the one change. What should the gold line say for 4 tickets after the lift?
Chunk 1's curse, hiding in tonight's spell: tickets is text, so tickets * 5 weaves 44444 instead of multiplying. The one change: tickets = int(input("How many tickets sold? ")). Re-run: Gold earned: 20. A silent curse never announces itself. You have to know what the answer should be, and that's what the schematic and your predictions are for.

Chunk 5 · The crystal-ball ritualE5-PRO-TR-?? ⚠

The debugger: predict-before-reveal, built into VS Code

All week you predicted, then revealed. VS Code has that ritual built in. In your lifted clinic.py: click in the gutter left of the total = tickets * 5 line so a red dot appears (a breakpoint). Then Run → Start Debugging, choose Python File, and answer the ticket question. The spell freezes at your dot, before that line runs.

THE RITUAL, AT THE FROZEN LINE:
1. PREDICT aloud: does the vessel  total  exist yet?
2. Look in the VARIABLES panel on the left.  (Reveal.)
3. Press F10 to step one line. PREDICT total's value first.
4. Look again.  (Reveal.)  Press F5 to let the spell finish.
Predict At the frozen moment, before F10, is total in the Variables panel or not? And after one F10 step, typing 4: what exact value appears beside it?
Not there. A vessel isn't born until its line runs, so you'll see tickets: 4 waiting but no total. One F10 step later, total: 20 appears. The crystal ball is your Grimoire trace table, drawn live by the wand itself. Use it on any curse the protocol can't corner.
Chunk 6 · Triage your MarvelE5-PRO-PD-?? ⚠

The Counter-Curse Log

Now your own Commission goes on the table. Run your Marvel. For every curse you meet, loud or silent, the log gets a row: Symptom | Predicted cause | Line found | The one change. If your Marvel runs clean, congratulations: push it further (a second gate, a second spell, more sparks) and log whatever the new magic stirs up. New features breed new curses, and the log is exactly where they belong.

SYMPTOM: my sparks freeze mid-air, but the window stays open
Predict Clinic warm-up: for that frozen-sparks symptom, which vessel would you watch first in the crystal ball: colors[i], ys[i], or FRAMES? Why that one?
ys[i]. Frozen sparks means positions stopped changing, and ys[i] is the vessel that should shrink every frame. If it isn't changing, trace one step upstream: is the update line still inside the loop? Did speeds[i] become 0? The symptom points to a vessel, and the vessel points to a line.
Hinge question · stop here until you're sureE5-PRO-TR-?? ⚠

The curse with no traceback

A spell runs top to bottom with no error at all, and it is still cursed.
How is that possible, and what do you compare its output against to prove it?

A silent curse: the wand did exactly what the code said, and the code said the wrong thing. 44444 gold; a missing Mira. You prove it by comparing what the spell does against what you said it should do, using your schematic, your predictions, your expected output. If you can't say what the right answer should be, you can't say the program is wrong.
Fast-finisher build · The Three-Door Quest

A pocket adventure, zero new syntax

Marvel lifted, log filled, time to spare? Build a terminal quest, three chambers with a choice in each, using only this week's magic:

1. A rich-tinted title and the seeker's name via input().
2. for room in range(1, 4): gives one pass per chamber.
3. In each chamber, ask left or right? and gate the reply with if pick in ["left", "l"]: and an else.
4. A rich-tinted victory line with the seeker's name.

from rich import print

print("[bold yellow]THE THREE DOORS OF THE VAULT[/bold yellow]")
name = input("Seeker's name: ")

for room in range(1, 4):
    print(f"[cyan]-- Chamber {room} --[/cyan]")
    pick = input("left or right? ")
    if pick in ["left", "l"]:
        print("A cold draft... but the way is clear.")
    else:
        print("Sparks shower down. You duck through!")

print(f"[bold yellow]{name} reaches the vault. The quest is complete![/bold yellow]")
Day 1's input, loop, and list-gate, painted with Day 2's stretch ink (run pip install rich if you skipped it). Three counted chambers on purpose: a quest with a known length needs no watch.
