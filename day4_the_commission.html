Chunk 1 · Read the Enchantment PanelE5-PRO-PD-?? ⚠

Every constant is a promise

The panel is the engine's contract. Before touching anything, say what each constant controls. This is Day 3's reading, aimed at tomorrow's changes.

SPARK_COLORS = [(240, 180, 41), (250, 245, 235), (196, 120, 255)]
SPARK_COUNT = 40
RISE_SPEED = 2

WAND_TIP_X = WIDTH // 2
WAND_TIP_Y = 400
SKY = (45, 27, 78)
Predict Which constant changes how crowded the sky is, and which changes how fast sparks climb? Which pair of numbers places the wand tip, and what would doubling RISE_SPEED look like?
SPARK_COUNT sets the crowd, RISE_SPEED the climb, and doubling it sends every spark up twice as fast per frame. The wand tip lives at (WAND_TIP_X, WAND_TIP_Y), Day 3's coordinates. The casting itself has no length constant: it runs until the brewer closes the window, and the frame vessel counts upward inside it so timed gates can fire. If you can predict a constant's effect, you have permission to change it. That is the whole test.
Chunk 2 · The Spell SchematicE5-ALG-PS-01

Draw the engine before you bend it

On your schematic sheet, draw the engine as boxes and arrows: setup at the top, the watched casting as a ring, and inside the ring the three beats: update the sparks, the respawn decision, draw. Label where SPARK_COUNT, RISE_SPEED, and ys[i] live. Your teacher signs it. Only then do you customize.

Predict Before drawing: which of the three beats holds Day 1's flat gate? Where on your schematic will your own changes land: inside the ring, in the panel, or both?
The respawn decision is the gate. And your Commission lands in both places: the color list, count, and speed live in the panel; the turn-of-night gate and your own spell fire inside the ring. A plan that shows where each change lives is what turns customizing from guessing into engineering, and it's the artifact this page exists to collect.

A decision inside the ring

Day 1's flat gate, cast on the sky. Find screen.fill(SKY) inside the frame loop and replace it with this. Predict, then run.

    if frame > 300:
        screen.fill((16, 8, 34))     # midnight falls halfway through
    else:
        screen.fill(SKY)
Predict At 60 frames per second, how many seconds into the casting does midnight fall? What would frame > 450 do instead?
Five seconds in: frame 300 at 60 frames per second. Move the gate to 450 and midnight falls at seven and a half seconds instead. One flat gate reading the frame vessel, and your Marvel has an act two. Mark on your schematic where it fires.

Chunk 4 · Worked change II: your own spellE5-PRO-PD-?? ⚠

A def in the book, a call in the ring

Yesterday you wrote draw_orb. The same move gives the wand a sigil: define near draw_wand, call right after draw_wand(screen).

def draw_sigil(surface):
    pygame.draw.circle(surface, (240, 180, 41),
                       (WAND_TIP_X, WAND_TIP_Y), 40, 2)

# ...inside the frame loop, right after draw_wand(screen):
    draw_sigil(surface=screen)
Predict What will the sigil look like, and where? The last number in the circle call is 2: given the ring you saw in draw_orb had no such number, what might it control?
A thin golden ring, radius 40, glowing around the wand tip. The final 2 is the line width: a circle drawn with width 0 (or none) is filled solid, like your orbs, and any other number draws just the outline that thick. Your own sigil should be yours: a ring, a crescent of circles, whatever you can draw with circles and lines. Update the schematic to show where it fires.
You should see 
The wandlight as before, with a thin gold ring
hovering around the tip while sparks rise through it.
Hinge question · stop here until you're sureE5-ALG-PS-01

The count inside the ring

Look at your schematic. The respawn decision sits inside the spark loop, which sits inside the frame ring.
In one frame, how many times does the respawn decision run? And across the first ten seconds of a casting, 600 frames?

40 times per frame, once per spark, and 24,000 times in those ten seconds (40 × 600). If you said once, your schematic is missing the loop inside the ring; redraw before the Commission. If you got both, you can see the whole machine at once, which is exactly what the schematic is for.
The Commission · due before you leave todayE5-ALG-PS-01

Your Marvel: a customized Wandlight

Deadline law Your Marvel runs by the end of Day 4. Tomorrow's Counter-Curse Clinic lifts curses; it doesn't host first castings. Worst case at day's end, revert to the clean engine plus your one safest change. Nobody walks into Day 5 with nothing running.

The four requirements. Each is a tool from this week, and house law holds: one change per run, predict before each.

1. Your colors. Rebuild SPARK_COLORS as your own list of 3–5 color tuples.
2. Your numbers. Change SPARK_COUNT and RISE_SPEED; predict each effect before the run.
3. Your turn of night. Chunk 3's gate, at a frame you choose and can defend.
4. Your own spell. Your own def, your own drawing, called in the ring, marked on the schematic.

At First Casting you present the Marvel with its signed schematic: point to the ring, the gate, and your spell as they fire.

Choose your exit gate

Middle · meets the standard

Your signed schematic shows sequence, the ring, both decisions, and your labeled variables, and your running Marvel matches it: your color list, your numbers, and the turn-of-night gate all wired in.

Stretch · exceeds the standard

Optional final flourish: grow the sky gate into a ladder with a dawn rung: after midnight, a second frame you choose and can defend where first light returns.
